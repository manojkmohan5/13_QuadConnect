What we keep

  • Your account: your username, your email address, and your password, which is stored only as a salted hash.
  • Your profile, once you set it up: your name, NetID, college and major, how you like to meet, your interests, the weekend times you are free, and a photo and a headline if you add them. Your Illinois email address is made from your NetID. A photo is kept as a smaller copy, without the details a phone stores in it, such as where it was taken. Every logged-in student can see your profile; the student list's download has your name, NetID, college, major, how you like to meet and your interests. None of it is public.
  • Your meetups: the weekends you sign up for, who you are matched with, whether you accept, when you check in, and your feedback. Only the students in a match, and staff, can see it. Your feedback is private to you and staff. Someone you met sees your Illinois email only if you both ask to stay connected.
  • If you continue with Google: Google sends us your name, your email address, a link to your profile picture and your Google account number. We ask Google for nothing else: not your contacts, files or calendar. We never see your Google password.
  • Cookies: one keeps you logged in, and one protects forms from being sent by other sites. There are no tracking, analytics or advertising cookies.

How we use it

To log you in, to show who is logged in, and to show your profile to other students so that you can be matched. Matching uses the interests and free times on student profiles. The students listed before anyone signed up are sample data.

What we share

Nothing about you is sold or shared. Our one public API, /api/summary/, publishes only how many students picked each interest, never names, NetIDs or email addresses. The site is hosted on PythonAnywhere, and Google handles "Continue with Google".

When you suggest a venue, its name, address and meeting note are sent to an AI service (Groq) that checks whether it looks like a public place; email addresses, phone numbers and links are removed first, and who sent the suggestion is never included. "Ask in plain English" searches are read by a language model on our own server and are not sent anywhere.

Your choices

You can change your profile at any time from "Your profile", beside your name. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email mm240@illinois.edu. To stop QuadConnect using your Google account, remove it from your Google account's third-party connections.

QuadConnect is a student project for INFO 490 MG at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, built by Team 13, The Connectors.
