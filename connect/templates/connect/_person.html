{% comment %} One student as a profile card, a list item of
    . Shared by the roster, the search results and "Ask in plain English". It is laid out as a profile is in any meetup app: a tall block of colour where the photo would be, the name over it, and the interests below. There are no photos, so the block holds what the app does know: the connection the student wants (blue for Friend Connect, purple for Squad Connect, and the badge says it) and their initials, large. The name is the card's one link, stretched over the whole card by the stylesheet, so the initials, the round arrow and the rest are not links of their own. The initials and the arrow only repeat the name and the link, so they are hidden from a screen reader; the stylesheet draws the initials from data-initials. Context ------- student StudentProfile from StudentProfile.objects.for_cards() match_q optional str, lower case: an interest containing it is marked as matching the search match_names optional set of interest names: these are marked as asked for, and the card says how many of them the student shares (student.shared, annotated by ai_search.find_students) A marked chip is orange and bold, and says why in words for a screen reader. A student's photo, if they gave one, fills the block in place of the initials (alt="": the name is right there), and their headline, if any, goes under the name. {% endcomment %}
